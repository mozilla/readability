The Lighthouse Keeper's Almanac

The Lighthouse Keeper's Almanac is a fictional drama about a retired sailor who takes a final posting at a remote lighthouse and begins recording the weather, the ships, and the small changes in the sea. The film was praised for its patient pacing and for its careful use of natural sound throughout the story.

Production took place over several months on a rocky stretch of coast. The crew built a working lamp room on location, and much of the dialogue was recorded in the actual tower so that the voices carried the echo of the stone walls. Critics noted how the setting became a character in its own right.

The film continues to be shown at retrospectives, and its soundtrack of waves, wind, and distant ship horns is often cited as an example of restrained and effective sound design in modern independent cinema.
